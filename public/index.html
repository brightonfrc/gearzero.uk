About Gear Zero

More than just a robot

Gear Zero is a FIRST Robotics Competition team based in Brighton, UK. We bring together students from a range of backgrounds to design, build and program robots, while developing skills that last a lifetime. Through teamwork, innovation and community, we strive to make a positive impact both in and out of the competition arena.
